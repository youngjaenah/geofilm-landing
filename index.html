Geotag photos from any camera

Even cameras without GPS: GeoFilm lines up each photo's capture time with the route you recorded while shooting. Film scans, mirrorless and older digital cameras all work.

GeoFilm Tracker

iPhone · Free download, one-time unlock for unlimited recording

  • One tap to record, visible on the Lock Screen
  • Starts by schedule or when you start moving
  • Time-correction screen suggests your camera's clock offset
  • Finished routes reach your Mac through iCloud

GeoFilm

Mac · Free

  • Matches locations by capture time
  • Per-camera clock offset and time zone
  • Writes to an XMP sidecar, a copy, or the original (with backup)
  • Check and fix on the map

No account. Photos and routes stay on your devices and in your own iCloud — nothing is sent to the developer.
